Alan Turing

Father of Theoretical Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence

Timeline of his life:

  • 1912 Born in London
  • 1936 Published "On Computable Numbers", introduced Turing Machine
  • 1939-1945 Broke Enigma cipher at Bletchley Park during WWII
  • 1950 Proposed the Turing Test
  • 1954 Died at age 41
  • 2009 UK Prime Minister Gordon Brown's official apology
